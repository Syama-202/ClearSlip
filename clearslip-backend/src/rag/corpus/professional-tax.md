## What professional tax is
Professional tax is a tax levied by state governments on people earning a salary or practising a profession. The employer deducts it from salary and pays it to the state. The rates and salary thresholds are set by each state, so the amount on your payslip depends on where you work.

## Maximum professional tax
The Constitution (Article 276) caps professional tax at ₹2,500 per person per year. Many states charge around ₹200 a month for higher salary bands; Maharashtra, for example, charges ₹200 a month and ₹300 in February for higher salaries so that the yearly total is ₹2,500. Several states and union territories, such as Delhi, do not levy professional tax at all.

## Professional tax and income tax
Professional tax paid can be deducted from salary income under section 16(iii), but only under the old tax regime. Under the new regime this deduction is not allowed.

## Checking your state's rates
Because slabs differ by state and change from time to time, check your state's commercial tax or professional tax department website for the current rates.
