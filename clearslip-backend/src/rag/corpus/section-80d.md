## Health insurance deduction limits
Section 80D allows a deduction for health insurance premiums: up to ₹25,000 a year for yourself, your spouse and children (₹50,000 if you are a senior citizen), plus up to ₹25,000 more for parents (₹50,000 if a parent is a senior citizen). Section 80D is available only under the old regime.

## Preventive health check-ups
Payments for preventive health check-ups up to ₹5,000 a year are allowed within the 80D limits, and can be paid in cash. Insurance premiums must be paid by a non-cash method.

## Employer group health insurance
Group health cover provided by your employer is not deductible under 80D for you, because you do not pay the premium. If your employer deducts a premium from your salary for family cover, check with HR whether it qualifies.
