## Who pays ESI
Employees' State Insurance (ESI) is a social-security scheme run by ESIC that gives medical care and cash benefits. It applies to employees whose gross wages are up to ₹21,000 a month (₹25,000 for persons with disability) in covered establishments. If your gross salary is above this limit, ESI is not deducted.

## ESI contribution rates
The employee contributes 0.75% of gross wages and the employer contributes 3.25% of gross wages. The employee's 0.75% appears as the "ESI" deduction on the payslip.

## What ESI gives you
Insured employees and their families get medical treatment at ESI hospitals and dispensaries, plus cash benefits such as sickness benefit, maternity benefit and disablement benefit. Each insured person gets an insurance number and an e-Pehchan card.
