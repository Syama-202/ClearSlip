## Interest on a home loan: section 24(b)
For a self-occupied house, interest paid on a home loan can be deducted up to ₹2,00,000 a year under section 24(b), but only under the old regime. Under the new regime, interest on a self-occupied house cannot be deducted.

## Principal repayment: section 80C
The principal part of the home-loan EMI counts towards the ₹1,50,000 limit of section 80C in the old regime, along with stamp duty and registration charges in the year paid.

## Telling your employer
Give your employer the lender's interest certificate during proof submission so it can reduce TDS if you are on the old regime. Otherwise you can claim it when filing your return.
