## What LTA covers
Leave Travel Allowance (LTA) is a salary component that is exempt from tax when used for travel within India while on leave. Only the travel cost (air, rail or road fare) for you and your family is exempt — not hotel stays, food or sightseeing. LTA exemption is available only under the old regime.

## How many trips you can claim
You can claim LTA exemption for two journeys in a block of four calendar years. The current block is 2022 to 2025 and the next block is 2026 to 2029. If you did not use a journey in a block, one unused journey can be carried over to the first year of the next block.

## Limits on the exempt amount
The exemption is limited to the actual travel cost, by the shortest route, up to economy airfare for air travel or AC first-class rail fare for rail. International travel does not qualify. Unused or unclaimed LTA is taxable as part of salary.

## Claiming LTA
Submit tickets and travel proof to your employer within its deadline. LTA cannot be claimed directly in the income tax return if the employer has not allowed it.
