## When gratuity is paid
Gratuity is a lump sum the employer pays when you leave a job after at least five years of continuous service (or earlier on death or disability). It is not part of the monthly payslip, but employers often include a gratuity provision — about 4.81% of basic — in the CTC.

## How gratuity is calculated
For employees covered by the Payment of Gratuity Act, gratuity = 15/26 × last drawn monthly basic plus DA × completed years of service. For example, with a last basic of ₹40,000 and 6 years of service, gratuity is 15/26 × 40,000 × 6 ≈ ₹1,38,462.

## Tax on gratuity
For private-sector employees, gratuity is tax-free up to ₹20 lakh over their career under section 10(10); any amount above that is taxable. Gratuity received by government employees is fully exempt.
