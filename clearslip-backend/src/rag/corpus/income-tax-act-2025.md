## The new Income-tax Act from 1 April 2026
The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961 from 1 April 2026. It applies from tax year 2026-27, which is the financial year 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. The new Act uses a single "tax year" in place of the old "previous year" and "assessment year". Income earned in FY 2025-26 is still taxed, and its return filed, under the 1961 Act.

## Income-tax rates for 2026-27 after Budget 2026
Budget 2026 left income-tax rates as they were. For 2026-27 the slabs, the ₹75,000 standard deduction and nil tax up to ₹12 lakh of taxable income (₹12.75 lakh for a salaried person) are the same as in FY 2025-26, and so are the old regime's slabs and limits.

## New section numbers for familiar deductions
The 2025 Act renumbers most sections, but the deductions themselves and their limits stay the same. For example, the ₹1,50,000 deduction for PF, PPF, ELSS, life insurance and tuition fees, known as section 80C, is section 123 of the new Act. Employers, HR forms and many guides still use the old section names such as 80C, 80D and 80CCD(1B), so either name may appear on your documents.
