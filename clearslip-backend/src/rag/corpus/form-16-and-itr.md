## What Form 16 is
Form 16 is the TDS certificate your employer gives you for salary. Part A shows the tax deducted and deposited against your PAN; Part B shows the salary breakup, exemptions, deductions and the tax computation. Employers must issue Form 16 by 15 June after the end of the financial year.

## Who should file an income tax return
You must file an income tax return if your income is above the basic exemption limit, and it is also needed to claim a refund of excess TDS. Filing every year also helps when applying for loans or visas.

## Which ITR form salaried people use
Most salaried individuals with income up to ₹50 lakh from salary, one house property and other sources such as interest can use ITR-1 (Sahaj). Others use ITR-2 or other forms depending on their income. Check the eligibility on the e-filing portal before filing.

## Due date and late filing
For salaried individuals whose accounts are not audited, the due date for filing the return is normally 31 July after the financial year ends, unless the government extends it. A belated return can be filed until 31 December with a late fee under section 234F of ₹5,000, or ₹1,000 if total income is up to ₹5 lakh.

## Matching Form 16 with your payslips
Before filing, compare Form 16 with your payslips and with Form 26AS/AIS. The salary and TDS figures should match; if they don't, ask your employer to correct its TDS return.
